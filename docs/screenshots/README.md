## Taking them

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>4</kbd>, then <kbd>Space</kbd>, and
click the overlay panel to capture just the window with its shadow. For a shot
that includes the game behind it, drag a region instead.

Crop out anything you would rather not publish — your character name, your
account handle, and the seller names in the listing table are all visible in a
full-width capture.

## A note on the game's art

These screenshots contain Path of Exile's artwork, which belongs to Grinding
Gear Games. Showing it to document a third-party tool is what every other tool
in this space does, but it is worth knowing that it is their art and not
yours, and that they can ask for it to come down.
